# References for `qrom-and-post-quantum-proof-modeling`

- **EASYPQC21** — [EasyPQC: Verifying Post-Quantum Cryptography](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3460120.3484567) (2021) — EasyPQC authors. `research-paper`. Extension of EasyCrypt for selected quantum-adversary reasoning patterns.
- **UNRUH17-QROM** — [Post-Quantum Security of Fiat-Shamir](https://eprint.iacr.org/2017/398) (2017) — Dominique Unruh. `research-paper`. Foundational QROM treatment of Fiat–Shamir under strengthened conditions.
- **DILITHIUM-EC23** — [Fixing and Mechanizing the Security Proof of Fiat-Shamir with Aborts and Dilithium](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/246) (2023) — Manuel Barbosa et al.. `research-paper`. Formalization uncovered and repaired a proof gap; includes a mechanized ROM proof in EasyCrypt.
- **MLKEM-EC24** — [Formally verifying Kyber Episode V: Machine-checked IND-CCA security and correctness of ML-KEM in EasyCrypt](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/843) (2024) — José Bacelar Almeida et al.. `research-paper`. Connects ML-KEM security, correctness, implementation equivalence, and constant-time Jasmin code.
- **SQUIRREL-PQ** — [A Logic and an Interactive Prover for the Computational Post-Quantum Security of Protocols](https://eprint.iacr.org/2022/401) (2022) — Cas Cremers, Alex B. Grilo, Sam Scott, and others. `research-paper`. PQ-BC/PQ-Squirrel soundness and mechanized post-quantum protocol case studies.
- **XMSS-EC23** — [Machine-Checked Security for XMSS as in RFC 8391](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/408) (2023) — Manuel Barbosa et al.. `research-paper`. EasyCrypt libraries and security proof for XMSS.
